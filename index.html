GOOD QUESTIONS. SIMPLE ANSWERS.

Your questions,
answered.

Get to know the plan options.

Which plan is right for me?

Choose Starter for occasional browsing, Explore for everyday use, or one of the larger data plans for heavier use. Review each plan’s data allowance and Broadband Facts before choosing.

Is roaming in Canada and Mexico included?

Explore, Premium, Unlimited, and Unlimited Plus list roaming in Mexico and Canada. Confirm the included talk, text, and data allowances and any restrictions before traveling.

When does my plan expire?

Plans follow a 30-day service period from the purchase date. Renew before the service period ends to avoid interruptions. Subscription renewals may work differently; confirm your renewal date when activating service.

Do 5GMobile plans include 5G coverage?

Yes, 5G is included with monthly plans. You need a compatible 5G-capable device and must be in a 5G coverage area to use it.

How do I check coverage in my area?

Confirm coverage for your ZIP code before activation, including the places where you live, work, and travel. View the coverage overview on this page, and confirm coverage for your specific plan before activation.

How do I order SIM cards?

Confirm SIM availability and ordering options with your retailer before purchasing a plan. SIM ordering is not yet available on this website.

How can I pay my bill?

Confirm available payment methods and renewal options with your retailer. Online bill payments are not yet available on this website.

Does 5GMobile offer unlimited talk, text, and data?

All plans include unlimited talk and text. Unlimited and Unlimited Plus include unlimited data, with plan-specific high-speed allowances and restrictions. Review the plan details and Broadband Facts before purchasing.

What type of phone works with 5GMobile?

Use an unlocked 4G LTE or 5G phone that is compatible with the service network. Confirm your specific phone’s compatibility before activation.

Can I use my phone as a hotspot?

All plans include hotspot data. Confirm your plan’s hotspot allowance and any restrictions before activation.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Number transfers are subject to eligibility. Have your current account details ready, and keep your existing service active until the transfer is complete.

Which plans include international calling?

Premium, Unlimited, and Unlimited Plus include calling to 100+ international destinations. Review the country list and confirm eligible number types and calling limits before activation.

How can I get help?

Contact the retailer that supplied your SIM or plan for activation and renewal assistance. Dedicated 5GMobile support contact details will be added here when available.
